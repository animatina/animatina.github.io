App Privacy Policy

animatina ("we") operates an internal tool, "animatina Poster", that uploads short videos produced by animatina to animatina's own social media accounts (TikTok, YouTube).

What we access. With your authorization, the tool uses the TikTok and YouTube APIs only to (1) upload our own videos to our own accounts as drafts or private videos, and (2) read basic statistics of those videos (views, likes, comments) to improve our content.

What we store. Access tokens issued by TikTok and Google are stored on a single computer in our workshop and are never shared with third parties. We do not collect, sell or share personal data of viewers or other users.

Retention and deletion. Tokens are deleted when the connection is removed. You can revoke access at any time from your TikTok or Google account settings.
