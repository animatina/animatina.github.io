App Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

animatina ("we") operates "animatina Poster", an internal tool that uploads short videos produced by animatina to animatina's own social media accounts (YouTube and TikTok). The tool is used only by animatina staff and is not offered to the public.

YouTube API Services

animatina Poster uses YouTube API Services. By using this tool you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Information accessed through YouTube API Services is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

What we access, use and store

  • YouTube: with the channel owner's authorization, the tool uploads our own videos to our own channel (title, description, tags and privacy status), reads the channel name to confirm which channel is connected, reads basic statistics of our own videos (views, likes, watch time) and reads comments on our own videos so that we can reply to them.
  • TikTok: with the account owner's authorization, the tool sends our own videos to our own TikTok account as drafts and reads the account's display name.
  • We do not collect, sell or share personal data of viewers or other users. Comments and statistics are used only to improve our own content and to answer customer questions.

Storage and security

Authorization tokens issued by Google and TikTok, and the statistics read through the APIs, are stored only on a single computer in our workshop in Japan. They are never shared with third parties. Statistics are kept for up to 24 months and then deleted.

Revoking access and deleting data

You can revoke the tool's access to your Google account at any time from the Google security settings page, and to your TikTok account from TikTok's "Manage app permissions" settings. When access is revoked, or upon request, we delete the stored tokens and data within 7 days.

Cookies

This tool does not place cookies on the devices of viewers or other users.
